Predicting and Managing Emotional and Productive Climate

A dashboard to reduce talent turnover in creative agencies.

Problem

Creative agencies suffer from high talent turnover and often find out only after the employee has already decided to leave.

Solution

A tool fed with employee data that shows who is at risk of leaving, so the agency can act in time with concrete improvements.

Tools

  • Google Sheets: employee database
  • Power BI: modeling and interactive dashboard

Data analyzed

  • Pending tasks and absences
  • Response time and tone in email/chat
  • LinkedIn activity
  • Detected emotion and climate survey (1-10)
  • Feedback received

What it shows

  • Turnover risk by department
  • Absences by department and feedback
  • Emotional climate by area
  • Traffic-light risk table by employee
  • Filters by department, tone, tasks and more

Busiest Airports by Passenger Traffic

Practice project: a comparative dashboard of global air traffic from 2018 to 2024.

Goal

Practice data visualization in Power BI by analyzing how passenger traffic has changed at the world's largest airports.

Tools

  • Excel: data preparation and organization
  • Power BI: modeling and interactive dashboard

What it shows

  • Map of the busiest airports in 2024
  • Top 10 airports from 2020 to 2024, with a trend line
  • Country comparison from 2018 to 2024
  • City-level evolution from 2018 to 2024

Key observations

  • A sharp drop in traffic in 2020, followed by recovery through 2024
  • The United States has the highest volume among the countries compared
